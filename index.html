How a catch-up works

Catch Up reads and ranks. It never sends, deletes, or files anything. Every reply stays yours to send.

01

Pick how far back

24 hours, 3 days, or 7. A long weekend, a week of deep work, or a holiday: the window decides how much gets pulled in.

02

Claude reads what's unread

Subjects, senders, and previews go out in one pass and come back ranked high to low, each with a plain-language summary of what the thread actually wants from you.

03

Reply to what matters

High-priority threads arrive with a draft reply ready to copy into Gmail. Everything low-priority stays folded away until you ask for it.

Small enough to check between things

The whole product is one popup in your toolbar: window picker, one button, and the result. It remembers your last catch-up, so closing the popup doesn't lose it.

✓

A digest first. One paragraph telling you what happened while you were gone.

✓

Ranked threads with a summary each, and a Gmail deep link to open any of them.

✓

Draft replies on the ones that need answering, one click to copy.

What it reads, and what it doesn't

A tool that reads your mail should be exact about what that means, so here it is without the hedging.

✓

Reads subject, sender, date and Gmail's own preview snippet of unread threads in your chosen window.

✓

Uses that once to write your digest, then discards it. No thread content is stored on our side, ever.

✕

Never opens full message bodies beyond the preview Gmail already shows in your inbox list.

✕

Never sends, deletes or files anything. Drafts are copy-paste only, by design.

✕

No ads, no trackers, no selling data, and your mail is never used to train models.
