3 free catch-ups a month, then €0.50 per catch-up or €3.99/month for unlimited.

Summaries and draft replies are AI-generated and can be wrong — always check the original email before relying on one, especially for anything important.

Drafts are copy-paste only. Catch Up never sends anything on your behalf.

Cancel anytime from the in-app "manage / cancel" link — no phone call, no dark patterns.

Provided best-effort, with no uptime guarantee — it's a small, independently-run tool.

01The service

Catch Up reads your unread Gmail threads for a time window you choose, and uses Anthropic's Claude to generate a priority ranking, short summaries, and — for higher-priority threads — draft replies you can copy into Gmail yourself.

!

AI output can be wrong. Priority ranking, summaries, and drafts are generated automatically and may misread tone, miss context, or misjudge importance. Catch Up is a triage aid, not a replacement for reading anything you actually need to act on. We're not responsible for decisions made solely on the basis of a summary or priority label.
